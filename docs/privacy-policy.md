# Privacy Policy – Night Shift Stories Poster

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Night Shift Stories Poster is a personal tool used by its owner to publish the owner's own original videos to the owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

**Data we access:** only the TikTok account authorized by the owner (basic profile info and permission to upload/publish videos). We do not access other users' data.
**Data we store:** an access/refresh token for the owner's account, kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository. We do not collect, sell, share or store personal data of any other person.
**Third parties:** videos are processed with AI services (text and voice generation) and stock footage providers; no personal data of viewers is sent to them.
**Revoking access:** the owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps and services).
