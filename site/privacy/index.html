Privacy Policy

Night Shift Stories Poster · Last updated 17 November 2026

What we access

Only the TikTok account that the operator authorizes: basic profile information and permission to upload and publish videos. We do not access any other user's data.

What we store

One access token for the operator's own account, kept as an encrypted secret in the operator's private code repository. We do not collect, sell or share personal data about anyone else, including viewers.

Third-party services

Story text and narration are produced with AI services, and background footage comes from stock video libraries. No viewer data is sent to these services.

Removing access

The operator can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services.
